⚙️ How Training Works

  • 1

    Feature Extraction

    For each video, optical flow magnitude is computed across frames to capture motion patterns — erratic motion, sudden bursts, sustained pressure movements.

  • 2

    Statistical Profiling

    6 motion features per video: mean, std, max magnitude, std of flow, 75th percentile, 90th percentile. These create a behavioral fingerprint.

  • 3

    Threshold Classifier

    A simple but effective threshold-based classifier is trained — no heavy ML libraries needed. Works fully offline.

  • 4

    Model Saved

    Model is saved as models/model.json. Accuracy is reported after training.
